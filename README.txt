CINEX NETWORKS PVT LTD - WEBSITE PACKAGE

Files:
- index.html: Home page
- about.html: Company introduction
- services.html: Service details
- contact.html: Contact details and enquiry form
- assets/css/style.css: Responsive styling
- assets/js/main.js: Mobile navigation and enquiry form behaviour
- assets/images/cinex-logo.png: Company logo supplied in the conversation

HOW TO PREVIEW:
1. Extract the ZIP file.
2. Open index.html in a modern browser.

BEFORE PUBLISHING:
1. Replace [Add your company phone number], [Add your official company email], [Add your office address], and business hours in contact.html and the footer.
2. In contact.html, replace data-email="YOUR_COMPANY_EMAIL" with your real company email address.
3. Replace any service text or images to match your exact business offering.
4. The contact form uses the visitor's default email app (mailto). To receive enquiries directly on a hosted website, connect a server-side form service or backend.
5. Some decorative service photos are loaded from Unsplash and require an internet connection.

DEPLOYMENT:
Upload the extracted folder contents to your hosting provider's public web directory (often public_html or www).
